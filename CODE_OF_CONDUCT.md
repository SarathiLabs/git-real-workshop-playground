# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Git Real is a classroom. Treat every participant as a colleague you will work with again.

## Expected

- Review the code, not the person.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming a mistake is carelessness.
- Credit ideas you reuse.
- Keep comments specific and kind.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or exclusion of any kind.
- Sharing someone else's token, grades, or private data.
- Force-pushing to shared branches you do not own.
- Merging your own PR into `main`.

## Reporting

Talk to a workshop instructor or organizer if something feels off. They can reassign reviews, pause a thread, or take a participant aside.

## Enforcement

Instructors may ask for a rewrite of a comment, reassign a review, or remove access to the playground repository.
